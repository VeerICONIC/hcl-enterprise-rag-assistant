Billing FAQ

Invoices

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.

Late payments

A late fee of 1.5% per month applies to overdue balances.

Refunds

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase. Requests must be sent to the billing team.

Payment methods

You can update your payment method from the Billing page in your account settings.
