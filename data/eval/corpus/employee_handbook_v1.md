# Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year. Unused annual leave may be carried over for up to 5 days into the next year.

### Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 days.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

## Expenses

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $40 per day. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.
